## Business Challenge
As the customer’s AWS and Kubernetes environment scaled, managing infrastructure reliability, operational visibility and day-to-day EKS operations became increasingly complex. The team needed to move from a reactive operating model to a proactive, structured cloud operations framework — with centralized monitoring, proactive alerting, EKS lifecycle management, incident response and ongoing AWS cost governance across a growing set of production workloads.
## Adamas Solution
Adamas implemented a comprehensive AWS Managed Services model centered on Amazon EKS. We established centralized observability using Amazon CloudWatch, Prometheus and Grafana, giving full visibility across EKS clusters, worker nodes, workloads and application health, and took on the ongoing operational responsibilities that come with running Kubernetes at scale.
- Cluster health, lifecycle and patch management
- Node health, capacity monitoring and planning
- Production workload monitoring and troubleshooting
- Structured incident management with defined escalation and runbooks
- Ongoing AWS cost governance and EC2 rightsizing
## Business Outcomes
Clear, centralized visibility across Kubernetes workloads and infrastructure health
Standardized EKS operations and lifecycle management
Structured incident response with clear escalation paths
Continuous AWS cost governance and optimization
Reduced day-to-day operational burden on the customer’s engineering team
A proactive, observable EKS operating environment — with the engineering team focused on the product, not the pager.
