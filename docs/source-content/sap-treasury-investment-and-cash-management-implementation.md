# SAP Treasury, Investment and Cash Management Implementation
Accelerate your treasury, investment, and cash management processes with streamlined implementation for optimized financial operations and enhanced efficiency.
[image: https://adamastechconsulting.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Frame-2617.png | alt=""]
## Business Overview
- Project Process Re-organization – An Indian Multinational Conglomerate – amongst the top 10 business houses in India.
- Its footprint stretches over a wide range of industries, spanning automobiles (two wheelers manufacturer and three wheelers manufacturer), home appliances, lighting, iron and steel, insurance, travel and finance. The group’s flagship company is ranked as the world’s fourth largest three and two wheeler manufacturer and the brand is well-known across several countries in Latin America, Africa, Middle East, South and South East Asia.
- Project was to have a harmonize and centralized Treasury process for entire group. Also, to improve cash collection, debt management reporting, the company chose SAP Business Warehouse powered by SAP HANA.
## Solutions Offered
### Implementation of SAP-TRM
Loans, investments (Bonds and Debentures), Fixed Deposits, Derivatives and Forward Contracts.
### Investment Management
Process for managing investments in Equity Shares, Mutual Fund, Fixed Deposits, Inter-Company Deposits, Bonds and Debentures.
### Cash Management
Mapping of Actuals for Cash Flow Planning for the entire group Reverse Integration with SAP-BPC – to get the actuals
### Inter-Company loans and investment
Limit Tracking for Fund and non-fund based process. Out of Box solution for Non-Fund based Limit as the same not supported in SAP.
## Technologies Leveraged
- MSAP S4 HANA on Cloud
- Treasury Management
- Bank Communication Management
## Business Benefits
## 30%
Improvement in cash / forecasting accuracy
## 20%
Decrease in DSO (Days Sale Outstanding) and DPO (Days Payable Outstanding)
## 60%
Reduction in manual reconciliation efforts and operational costs
## 99%
Faster reporting (from 8 hours to 5 seconds)
## More Benefits
- End-to-End tracking of Loans and investment portfolios in SAP with automatic accounting compliant to IndAs.
- Customized Limit tracking for the client providing the complete visibility of limits across the various companies, line of business and loan portfolio (both for Fund and non-fund based)
- End to End tracking of the Loan and investment portfolio from the system.
- Compliant accounting and recording system with respect to IndAs rules
- Up to 5-10% higher returns on invested cash balances through optimized investment decisions and better yield management.
- End to end Limit monitoring available in SAP, with automatic interest accrual, Mark-to-Market and accounting
- Achieved 20-30% decrease in audit preparation time and associated compliance expenses due to streamlined processes and enhanced transparency
- Real-time Cash Position and Liquidity Forecast available across the company enabling to plan for the Fund requirement/utilization.
- Generated additional cash flow equivalent to 2-5% of annual revenue through accelerated collections and optimized payment terms.
