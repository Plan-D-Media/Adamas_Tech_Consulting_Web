# Resilient Backup & Recovery with AWS
City Engineering College in Bengaluru serves ~12,000 students, 800+ faculty, and 25 departments. Its Laravel-based University Management System (UMS) and exam portals were struggling with legacy infrastructure, making it difficult to scale academic services securely and cost-effectively
[image: https://adamastechconsulting.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Frame-2801-1.png | alt=""]
## The Challenges
- Growing demand for online exams and e-learning strained legacy servers.
- No automated backup, risking exam/academic data loss.
- Security and privacy concerns with limited auditability.
- High IT workload and costs due to on-prem refresh cycles
## Why AWS
- Highly available storage and backup.
- Strong encryption and IAM for secure access.
- Cost optimization through storage classes.
- Proven disaster recovery with restore testing
## The Solution
### Amazon S3
Unified storage for exam data, student files, learning resources.
### Amazon RDS (MySQL)
Multi-AZ backend with PITR and cross-region copies.
### AWS Backup
Centralized orchestration for S3, RDS, and EBS workloads.
### IAM & KMS
Role-based departmental access with enforced encryption.
### CloudWatch & SNS
Monitoring, alerts, and workload observability.
### Restore Testing:
Scheduled drills validated RPO/RTO and data integrity
## Benefits Delivered
## 55%
storage cost savings compared to on-prem
## 40%
IT workload reduction through automation
## RPO improved
To 30 minutes with tested recovery.
## 60%
faster exam data recovery.
## 99.9%
UMS uptime, ensuring uninterrupted student access
## Outcome
City Engineering College now operates with a secure, scalable, and resilient AWS-powered academic system. Backup automation and restore validation protect exam integrity and improve student/teacher experience, while cost savings free resources for innovation.
