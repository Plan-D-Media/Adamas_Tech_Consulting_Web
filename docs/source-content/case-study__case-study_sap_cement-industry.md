## Business Challenge
As SAP became increasingly critical to business operations, the customer needed to strengthen business continuity and disaster recovery readiness across its AWS environment. This meant establishing a structured approach to SAP HANA recovery, DR infrastructure readiness, failover and failback procedures, and RTO/RPO validation. Without consolidated monitoring, it was difficult to proactively identify issues across SAP, infrastructure, storage, networking, backups and replication health before they affected the business.
## Adamas Solution
Adamas designed and operates a comprehensive AWS Managed Services framework spanning SAP disaster recovery, infrastructure monitoring, incident management and infrastructure administration. For DR, the solution uses SAP HANA System Replication between primary and DR environments, backed by continuous replication monitoring, documented recovery runbooks and periodic validation — with RTO/RPO testing built in as a repeatable process rather than a one-time exercise.
- Centralized monitoring across EC2 infrastructure, storage and networking
- SAP application and SAP HANA health, replication and backup monitoring
- Documented recovery runbooks and failback procedures
- Periodic DR validation exercises and recovery testing
- EC2 lifecycle management, OS patching and AWS Backup
- Ongoing IAM, VPC and infrastructure health reviews
## Business Outcomes
Stronger business continuity and disaster recovery readiness
Structured SAP HANA replication and recovery management
24×7 monitoring across AWS and SAP environments
Proactive alerting and standardized incident response
Improved infrastructure and cloud governance
Reduced operational overhead for the customer’s IT and SAP teams
A tested, recovery-ready SAP environment on AWS — validated on a schedule, not assumed until an outage.
