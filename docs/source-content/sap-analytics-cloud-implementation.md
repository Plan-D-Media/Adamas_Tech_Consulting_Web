## More Benefits
- Organization achieved up to 25-30% faster decision-making cycles due to real-time access to integrated data from SAP S/4HANA Cloud
- State-wise Cost Sheets using dynamic calculation logics considering state-wise variations.
- Planned Financial Statements at Plants, State and Country level by considering Planned values using system and linking them with Financial GL codes.
- With increased revenue through Data-driven Insights, business realized 5-10% increase in revenue through targeted marketing campaigns, optimized pricing strategies, and improved customer retention initiatives.
- Significant reduction in Planning time because of seamless data integration, inbuilt logic in the system, and higher accuracy because of minimal manual intervention.
- Overall P&L Report by consolidating data from Three Models, 5 Centralized Reports, and 27 State-wise Cost-sheets.
- Scenario analysis using data on different versions of Actual, Plan and Forecast.
- Significantly accelerated several steps in its financial close and forecasting cycles.
- Ensured authorized access by assigning roles to regional and admin users, setting up DAC at Model Level, and viewing, editing, and deleting rights at the story level
