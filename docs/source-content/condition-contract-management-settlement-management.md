# Condition Contract Management (Settlement Management)
Condition Contract Management in SAP Settlement Management enables efficient handling of complex rebate agreements, settlements, and contract-based revenue management.
[image: https://adamastechconsulting.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Frame-2865.png | alt=""]
## Business Overview
- The client is one the major CPG industry giant who are using the rebate process in ECC. Now they have moved into SAP S4 HANA cloud edition.
- The Company that sells its products through various distributors and resellers. The company offers volume-based rebate agreements to incentivize higher sales volumes from these channel partners.
- Complex Rebate Agreements: The company manages a variety of rebate agreements with different terms and conditions based on sales volumes, product categories, and customer types.
- Manual Processes: Previously, rebate calculations were done manually using spreadsheets, leading to errors, disputes, and delayed settlements.
- Lack of Visibility: Limited visibility into rebate accruals, payments, and performance across different regions and product lines made it challenging to track profitability and assess the effectiveness of rebate programs.
## Solutions Offered
### Condition Contract Management (CCM)
Defines rebate agreements with distributors based on specific criteria (e.g., sales volume, product category).
### Automated Rebate Calculation
Sales data from SAP S/4HANA Sales & Distribution (SD) automatically triggers rebate calculations based on the pre-defined conditions in the CCM.
### Real-time Visibility
Distributors can access a self-service portal to view their rebate accruals and estimated payouts in real-time.
### Simplified Dispute Resolution
A transparent audit trail facilitates faster and more efficient resolution of any discrepancies in rebate calculations.
## Technologies Leveraged
- SAP S4 HANA on Cloud
- CCM
- SD, FI, PP, MM, Analytics

## More Benefits
- Accuracy and Efficiency: Automated rebate calculations contributed immensely towards reducing errors and disputes, improving accuracy in financial reporting and compliance with rebate agreements.
- Cost Savings: By streamlining manual processes, the company achieved in reducing administrative costs associated with rebate management by 20% in the first year itself.
