# SAP Fiori based Mobility Application for Supply Chain and Logistics
SAP Fiori-based mobility application enhances supply chain and logistics by providing real-time insights, process automation, and streamlined operations
[image: https://adamastechconsulting.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Frame-2883.png | alt=""]
## Business Overview
- A Fortune 500 (India) company, synonymous with quality products & affordable prices. It manufactures slippers, sandals , sports and casual shoes.
- Having a PAN India distribution footprint, Operating 350+ strong network of own retail outlets with availability on all major Ecommerce portal as well.
- Business challenges
- End to End Tracking of products (Pairs) from Plants to Retail Outlets.
- Maintain Inventory and settle the claims Individual store wise. Better resource planning and optimization
- Cost optimization on inventory and logistics
- Dashboard overview reporting with end to end visibility
## Solutions Offered
### Seamless Scanning
Implemented UI5 Applications in FIORI devices (Zebra) to Scan HU.
### Automated Processing
Post Scanning of HU’s users Submitted the data through FIORI app Data gets posted in the backend and STO, Invoicing gets generated along with that user receives mail on Successful posting.
### Nationwide Deployment
Implementation of 40,000 Zebra Fiori devices across PAN India including Plants, Warehouses and Retail Outlets.
### Streamlined Operations
- Following are the processes which were implemented:-
- Entry Gate Pass
- GRN Prepack
- Stock Inward
- Batch Put Away
- Dispatch Sorting / Allocation
- Dispatch
- Out Gate Pass
## Technologies Leveraged
- AI Based Cloud Solution
- SAP Cloud Integration
## Business Benefits
## 15%
Reduction in inventory costs due to improved visibility and planning
## 30%
savings in efforts by better resource planning and allocation
## 45%
Reduction in overall operations and logistics efforts at warehouse levels
## More Benefits
- Transparent and seamless goods movement with Real-time Tracking of Inventory.
- Contact-free identification, control and information exchange
- Improved process efficiencies leading automation and cost Optimization.
- Quantitively benefit on right resource allocation and optimization
- Easy stowage for a smooth Material Movement/Logistics process, turning conventional warehouses and production houses into Smart productive units.
- Easy Inventory allocation/relocation based on pre-defined HU
- Optimized Packaging / Warehouse Processes
- Easy Batch Tracking, Easy Reverse Logistics and avoidance of manual data entry
- Faster and improved decision making at all operation and management levels.
- Great User experiences and business ease of use
