# Financial Group Consolidations with SAP BPC and BW HANA Technology Solutions
Achieve seamless financial group consolidations using SAP BPC and BW HANA, enhancing accuracy, speed, and transparency in financial reporting.
[image: https://adamastechconsulting.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Frame-2827.png | alt=""]
## Business Overview
- Customer is the largest conglomerate of Vietnam with diversified interests
- More than 50 legal entities
- The group is present into Real Estate, Retail, Hospitality, Education, Hospitals, Automobile etc.
## Solutions Offered
### Multiple GAAP reporting
Implementation of SAP BPC for Planning and Consolidation
### Multiple GAAP reporting
It ensured compliance with various accounting standards, providing accurate financial statements across different regulatory frameworks globally.
### Revenue Projection
Revenue Forecasting based on Existing students, new students, extra- courses
### Cashflow Projection
Cash-flow forecasting based on the school fee payment schedule (including advance and instalment payments)
### Data Integration
Data loading from 10 SAP instances to a centralized SAP BPC consolidation system
### Multi-lingual reporting
It enabled the business to generate reports in multiple languages, improving global communication and ensuring compliance with local regulations
### Planning Automation
Automation of End-to-End Planning process from Revenue Planning, Opex Planning to P&L and Cash Flow Planning
### Notes Automation
Automation of complex Notes to Accounts from SAP ERP
## Technologies Leveraged
- SAP BPC
- SAP BW on HANA
## Business Benefits
## 80%
Faster insights gained at critical times, freeing up senior team members
## 92%
Faster transfer of ledger reporting
## 78%
Faster budget generation (115 days to 22 days)
## 97%
Of overall data volume was cut by ¾
## More Benefits
- Integrated Financial Reporting resulted consistency and accuracy in financial reporting across the entire organization.
- Unified Chart of Accounts simplified the consolidation process and facilitates comparative analysis of financial performance across different business units.
- Comprehensive Intercompany eliminated double counting and ensured compliance with accounting standards such as IFRS or local GAAP.
- Advanced Currency Translation supported the conversion of financial data into a common reporting currency, facilitating global financial consolidation and reporting.
- Significant reduction in MIS time (consolidation time) because of seamless data integration
- Detailed inter-company reconciliation (transactional level)
- Real-time Consolidation and Reporting eliminated the need for batch processing and reduced the time-to-close financial periods. This agility in reporting supported faster decision-making and responsiveness to market dynamics.
- Automation of key consolidation processes such as calculation and amortization of Goodwill, NCI
- Visibility into school-wise and overall cash-flow based on the fee payment schedule
- Profitability analysis based on city/curriculum/ courseware
- Higher accuracy because of minimal manual intervention
- Single source of truth
- Tight security and access control on sensitive financial information
