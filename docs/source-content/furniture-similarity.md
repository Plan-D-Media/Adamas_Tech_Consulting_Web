# Furniture Match: AI-Driven Furniture Recognition and Matching System
Leverage AI to identify and match furniture styles, ensuring seamless design coordination and enhanced interior aesthetics.
[image: https://adamastechconsulting.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Frame-2685.png | alt=""]
## Challenge
Develop a system to identify similar furniture through images and descriptions, streamlining procurement, manufacturing, design, and cost estimation processes by leveraging historical data, and providing recommendations based on analyses.
## Solution
Cosine similarity calculation is used to compare encoded descriptions and picture attributes, enabling accurate furniture matching and producing a weighted score for similarity evaluation. The task was completed by following these steps:
- Precise comparisons are ensured by weighted scoring and sophisticated encoding methods for pictures and descriptions. successful matching of database objects with user photos.
- Gives users suggestions based on relevancy.
- All things considered, a more dependable and efficient furniture matching method.
