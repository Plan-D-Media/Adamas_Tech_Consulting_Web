# SAP S4HANA Business Partners Creation with Workflow Management Functionalities
Streamline business partner creation in SAP S4HANA with integrated workflow management, enhancing efficiency and control across your operations.
[image: https://adamastechconsulting.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Frame-2845.png | alt=""]
## Business Overview
- The client is one of the global CPG (Consumer Packaged Goods) giant with very complex business processes.
- The master data management is a very key parameter for their success. They are using the Workflow management extensible to get the maximum benefit out of the Business Partner Approval Process.
- They have moved from ECC 6.0 to S4 HANA Private cloud.
- In ECC for the client there was no approval process when any kind of master data was created. Now while creating the master data, they are having the approval system as well they are getting the facility for having the decision management capabilities as well as the Live Process Insights of the Business Partner Creation Approval Process.
## Solutions Offered
### Partner Automation
The client is using the Business Partner Creation Approval Process content package for S/4HANA Cloud which enables to automate business partner creation in a flexible and transparent way.
### Process Flexibility
It provides flexibility in executing a specific variant of the process depending on attributes like business partner category, etc.
### Decision Management
The business is also using the Decision Management capabilities in the workflow management.
## Technologies Leveraged
- SAP Analytics Cloud on HANA as a Tool for Automation
- SAP BW 4 HANA as Source System
